How your money is protected

Your deposits sit with ING Belgium and fall under the deposit protection that applies in Belgium. You receive the exact cover, the limit and the conditions in writing when you open your account.

You also see what we do with your data, and what we do not do with it, in the privacy statement that comes with the account.

  • Deposit protection applies to your deposits, with the limit and conditions set by law.
  • Every account comes with the terms, the price list and the privacy statement in writing.
  • You decide who can access your account, and you can change that when you want.

Frequently asked questions

Is ING a Belgian bank?
Yes. ING Belgium is a Belgian bank with branches and advisors in the country, and an app you can use wherever you are. The registered details are in the legal information you receive with your account.
How do I know which pack fits me?
Start with what you do each month: pay in shops, transfer money, travel, hold a second card. The comparison above shows the monthly fee, the card and what is included. If you are still unsure, an advisor will go through the packs with you before you sign anything.
What happens to my money if something changes?
Your deposits fall under the deposit protection that applies in Belgium. The exact cover, the limit and the conditions are set out in the documents you receive when you open your account, and you can ask us to explain them.
Do I have to do everything online?
No. You can open and manage your account in the app, and you can call us or visit a branch when you want to speak to a person. It is the same account either way.
Where can I see every fee and rate?
The price list holds every fee and rate that applies, including the ones we do not repeat here. You can read it before you open an account, and you receive it with your account afterwards.
