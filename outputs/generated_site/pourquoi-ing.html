Digital when you want it, human when you need it

Most days you want to check a balance, pay someone or move money without waiting in a queue. The ING app is built to do that on your phone, in a few taps.

Some moments need a person: a mortgage, a first investment, a payment that has gone wrong. For those, our advisors and branches are still here, by phone, in person or by video.

  • Pay, transfer and check your balance in the app
  • Reach an advisor in a branch, by phone or by video call
  • The same account and the same people, whichever way you bank

Frequently asked questions

Is ING a Belgian bank?
Yes. ING Belgium is a Belgian bank with branches across the country and its own Belgian banking licence. The exact wording of our licence and supervision is set out in [legal text to be confirmed].
Can I still speak to a person?
Yes. You can call us, book an appointment in a branch or ask for a video call. If you prefer not to use the app, you can do your everyday banking at a branch.
What happens if I lose my card?
Block it yourself in the app in a few taps. You can order a replacement at the same time, and the blocked card stays blocked.
Is my money protected if something happens to the bank?
Deposits are covered by the Belgian deposit protection scheme up to [MONTANT] per person per bank, under the conditions of that scheme. Full details: [legal text to be confirmed].
What does the app cost?
The app comes with your account. Any fee for a specific product or service is shown with that product, together with its conditions.
