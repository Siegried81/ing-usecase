Frequently asked questions

Is ING a Belgian bank?
Yes. ING Belgium is a Belgian bank with its head office in Brussels, serving customers across the country through the app, the website, the phone and our branches.
What does free mean when ING uses the word?
It means the service costs you nothing inside the conditions we state next to it. If a free service depends on holding a pack, keeping a minimum balance or staying with us for a period, you read that condition on the same line as the word free.
How do I reach a person?
Message us from the app, call us, or book an appointment with an advisor at a branch. You choose the moment, and you keep the same advisor for the follow-up.
What happens to my money if something goes wrong?
Deposits held with ING Belgium are covered by the protection that applies in Belgium, up to [MONTANT] per person, per bank. The full conditions are set out in the legal information provided with your account.
Can I bank in English?
Yes. You can use the app, the website and your documents in English, French or Dutch, and you can change your choice at any time.
