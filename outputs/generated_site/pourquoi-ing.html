Banking on your phone, and a person when you want one

Everyday banking sits in the ING Banking app. You can check your balance, make a payment, block your card and see what you spent. The same account works on ing.be if you prefer a larger screen.

When you want a person, you do not have to look far. You can call us, chat with us from the app, or book an appointment at a branch you choose.

  • Pay, transfer and check your balance in the app or on ing.be
  • Block or unblock your card yourself
  • Reach an advisor by phone, by chat or in a branch
  • Choose the branch where you want to be seen

Frequently asked questions

What does ING stand for in Belgium?
ING is a Belgian bank with branches across the country and an app for everyday payments. You choose the channel that suits you: app, website, phone or branch.
Is my money safe at ING?
Deposits are covered by the Belgian deposit guarantee scheme, up to [MONTANT] per person, per bank. The protection that applies to your account is set out in the documents you receive when you open it.
How long does opening an account take?
Opening an account takes [DUREE], including the identity check. You have the steps in front of you before you start, and you can pause and come back.
Can I still speak to someone?
Yes. You can call us, chat with us from the app or book an appointment at a branch you choose. The app shows the current opening hours.
What does ING do with my data?
We use your data to run your account, to confirm who is asking us to do what, and to meet our legal duties. We do not sell it. The privacy statement you receive at account opening explains this in full.
