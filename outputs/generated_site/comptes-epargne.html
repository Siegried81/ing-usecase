Put money aside, keep it within reach

Saving should not mean locking your money away for months. You decide when money goes in and when it comes out, and you can move it in the app in a moment, without a notice period.

That is what this account is for: the deposit on a first home, a car that finally needs replacing, or simply a month that turns out cheaper than expected. Your money keeps earning while it stays yours to move.

  • Take money out when you want, no notice period.
  • Follow your balance and your interest in the ING app.
  • Set a monthly transfer once and let it run.
  • Prefer to talk it through? Visit a branch.

Frequently asked questions

What rate do I get on my savings?
A base rate of [TAUX] on your balance, plus an extra [TAUX] once your money has been in the account for [DUREE]. Both rates and their conditions are set out above.
Can I take my money out whenever I want?
Yes. There is no notice period. You can transfer money out in the ING app or at a branch whenever you need it.
How do I open a savings account?
Open the ING app, choose Savings accounts and confirm your identity. You need your identity card and your phone, or your card reader. You can also open the account at a branch.
Is my savings money protected?
Savings held with ING are covered up to [MONTANT] per person per bank under the Belgian deposit protection rules.
Can I change how much I save each month?
Yes. You set up a monthly transfer yourself and change or stop it in the app at any time.
