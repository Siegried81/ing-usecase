What the account costs you

There is no account fee. You pay nothing to open the account, nothing to keep it, and nothing to pay money in or take money out.

You pay no fee for transfers between your own ING accounts. For any service outside the savings account itself, the price is shown in the price list before you use it.

Interest is paid on the balance in the account. Tax on that interest follows the rules set by the Belgian government, and your annual statement shows the figures applied to you.

  • No account fee, for as long as you hold the account.
  • No fee to deposit money or to withdraw it.
  • Any price for an extra service is shown to you before you take it.

Your money stays yours

You can take money out whenever you want. There is no notice period and no lock-in.

If you take money out before the [DUREE] qualifying period is complete, you keep the base rate on that money but you do not receive the fidelity premium for that period. That is the only condition on access, and it applies to everyone.

You move money between your ING accounts yourself, in the app or on ing.be, and you see the balance change straight away.

Frequently asked questions

What rate does the savings account pay?
A base rate of [TAUX] on your full balance, plus a fidelity premium of [TAUX] on the money that stays in place for [DUREE]. Both rates are in your account terms, and you see the interest on your statement when it is paid.
Can I take my money out when I want?
Yes. There is no notice period and no lock-in. If you withdraw before the [DUREE] qualifying period ends, you keep the base rate on that money but you lose the fidelity premium for that period.
Does the account cost anything?
No. There is no account fee, and there is no fee to deposit or withdraw money. If you later ask for a service outside the savings account itself, the price is shown in the price list before you take it.
What do I need to open the account?
Your e-ID or itsme, your national register number, and an account to transfer your first amount from. You can do it in the ING app, on ing.be, or in a branch.
