Term account or savings account?

Both hold money you are not using right now. The difference is how you want to use that money.

A savings account keeps your money reachable and its rate can change. A term account holds your money for a fixed term and pays a rate that is fixed for that same term.

  • Term account: rate fixed at [TAUX] for [DUREE]; your money stays in until the end date.
  • Savings account: rate can change; you take your money out when you want.
  • Save for something you may need soon: a savings account.
  • Set money aside for a date you already know: a term account.

What happens when the term ends

We contact you before the end date. You then choose: pay the money out, or start a new term at the rate available on that day.

Nothing is decided for you without a reminder first, and you always see the new rate before you agree to it.

  • Pay the money out to the account you choose.
  • Start a new term at the rate of that moment.
  • Change the amount if your plans have changed.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add money during the term?
No. You pay in one amount when you open the account and it stays there for the whole [DUREE]. If you want to keep saving in the meantime, a savings account keeps that money reachable.
What if I need the money before the term ends?
A term account is meant to stay untouched until the end date. If your plans change, contact us and we will explain what closing early means for you. The conditions are set out in the [LEGAL TEXT].
How do I know which rate I get?
Your rate depends on the term you choose and the amount you deposit. You see it on screen before you confirm, and it then stays fixed for the whole term. An example rate is [TAUX].
Is my money protected?
Deposits held with ING in Belgium are covered by the Belgian deposit protection scheme, up to the limit set by law. The exact conditions are set out in the [LEGAL TEXT].
How long does it take to open a term account?
A few minutes. You choose the amount and the term, see your rate, and confirm in the app with your ID.
