How your term account works

You open the account, transfer your amount, and the term starts on the date you and ING agree. From that date the rate and the end date are fixed in your agreement. Nothing about them changes while the term runs.

On the end date we pay your capital and the interest into the account you chose. There is no window that closes and no date you have to beat: the rate and the term in your agreement are the ones that apply to you, for as long as that agreement runs.

Term account or savings account?

A savings account keeps your money reachable. You can take it out when you need it, and the rate can change over time.

A term account works the other way round. You commit the amount for a period you agree in advance, and in return the rate stays put for that period.

If a rate that does not move for [DUREE] suits your plan, a term account does the job. You start from [MONTANT], at [TAUX] for the term you choose.

  • Term account: fixed rate [TAUX], fixed term [DUREE]

What happens at the end of the term

On the end date in your agreement, we pay your capital and the interest into the account you chose. If you already told us what you want to do with the money, we follow that instruction.

If your plans change before the end date and you want to know your options, contact us. We explain the conditions that apply to your term account and what closing it early would mean in your case, before you decide anything.

Frequently asked questions

Is the rate really fixed for the whole term?
Yes. The rate of [TAUX] applies from the start date to the end date in your agreement. It does not follow the market while your term runs, and it is not adjusted along the way.
What is the minimum amount I need to open one?
You can open a term account from [MONTANT]. The exact minimum for the term you choose is confirmed to you before you open the account.
Can I take my money out before the end date?
A term account is meant to stay in place until the end date. If you want to know what closing it early would mean in your case, ask us before you open it and we will explain the conditions that apply to your term.
How do I open a term account?
You open it online from your ING account, and an ING branch can help you if you prefer. Have your identity document and your account details with you when you start.
What happens when the term ends?
Your capital and the interest are paid into the account you chose on the end date in your agreement. You can tell us in advance what should happen next, or decide at that moment.
