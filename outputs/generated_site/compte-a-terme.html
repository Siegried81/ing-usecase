What happens to your money

You move money into a term account and leave it there for the term you picked. The rate does not move while your money is with us, so you know the amount you get at the end before you start.

Term account or savings account

A savings account keeps your money available. You pay in and take out when you want, and the rate can change while your money is there. That flexibility is the point.

A term account works the other way. You choose a term of [DUREE], your rate of [TAUX] is fixed from the start, and your money stays in until the end date. Nothing moves in either direction.

Many customers hold both: a savings account for money they may need, and a term account for money they will not touch for a while.

  • Want to stay flexible? A savings account fits better.
  • Want to know your rate today and leave the money alone for [DUREE]? A term account fits better.
  • Both are opened in the same app, and you can move between them when a term ends.
