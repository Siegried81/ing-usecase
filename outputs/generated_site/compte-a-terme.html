Term account or savings account?

A term account gives you a fixed rate [TAUX] for a fixed period [DUREE]. In return, your money stays in the account until the term ends.

A savings account gives you a variable rate [TAUX] and you can take your money out when you need it, within the rules of that account.

If you will not need the money for a while and you want to know what you earn, a term account fits. If you want to stay flexible, a savings account fits.

  • Fixed rate for a fixed term: term account.
  • Variable rate, money reachable: savings account.
  • You can split your savings across both.

What if I need my money before the term ends?
Your money stays in the account until the term ends. If you think you may need it earlier, talk to us before you open the account and we will explain the options that apply.
What happens at the end of the term?
You receive the interest built up over the term. You can then take your money out, open a new term at the rate of that moment, or move it to a savings account.
