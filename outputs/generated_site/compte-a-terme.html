Term account or savings account?

Both are ways to put money aside. The difference is what you want the money to do.

A savings account stays flexible. You pay in and take out when you want, and the rate on it can change. A term account fixes both the term and the rate, so you trade access for certainty.

  • Term account: rate fixed at [TAUX] for [DUREE], money stays put until the end date
  • Savings account: rate currently [TAUX], money stays reachable and the rate can move
  • Choose the term account when you will not need the money for [DUREE]
  • Choose the savings account when you want to keep access
