What can go wrong

An investment can fall as well as rise. You can get back less than you put in. That is the trade-off for a potentially higher return than a savings account pays.

Money you may need in the next few years is usually better kept in a savings account.

The protection that applies to savings accounts does not automatically apply to investments. Read the conditions before you decide, or ask us to go through them with you.

Frequently asked questions

How much do I need to start?
You can start from [MONTANT]. There is no need to invest a large sum at once, and you can add more later.
Can I take my money out?
Yes. You can sell your investment and withdraw the money. It usually takes [DUREE] before it reaches your account, and the value at that moment is the value you receive.
What happens if the market falls?
The value of your investment falls with it. You only turn that into a loss if you sell at that moment. This is why we ask how long you can leave your money invested.
Is my investment guaranteed?
No. The value can go down as well as up. The protection that applies to savings accounts does not automatically cover investments. Ask us for the details, or read them in the conditions.
Where can I see the costs?
You see every cost before you confirm your investment, and you can find them again in the ING app at any time.
