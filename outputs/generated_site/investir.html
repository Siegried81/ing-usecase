What risk means for your money

An investment can go down as well as up. There is no guaranteed return, and you can get back less than you put in.

The risk level you choose sets how much movement you accept. Lower risk usually means smaller swings and a lower expected return. Higher risk works the other way.

Invest money you will not need for the term you choose. Keep a buffer in a savings account for the short term.

  • The value of your investment can fall as well as rise.
  • There is no guaranteed return and no guaranteed capital.
  • Past results say nothing about future ones.

Frequently asked questions

How much do I need to start?
You can start from [MONTANT]. There is no maximum, and you can change your amount later.
Can I lose money?
Yes. An investment can fall in value and you can get back less than you put in. There is no guaranteed return.
How do I know which risk level fits me?
We ask about your goal, your horizon and how you react when a value drops. That points to a level. The final choice is yours.
