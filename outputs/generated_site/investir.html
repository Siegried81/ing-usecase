The risk, in plain words

Investing is not saving. The value of what you invest in goes down as well as up, and you can get back less than you put in.

Before you confirm anything, you see the risk level of what you have chosen and how it has moved over [DUREE]. Past results are not a promise of what comes next.

Never invest money you may need soon. Keep your emergency savings separate, and start with an amount you can leave alone for a while.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a large amount to start?
No. You can start with [MONTANT] and add to it when you want. A monthly plan from [MONTANT] works the same way, and you can stop it at any time.
What happens if the market falls?
The value of your investment falls with it. You keep the same investment and nothing forces you to put in more than you planned. You can pause your plan and wait, or sell whenever you choose.
What does investing cost me?
You pay [TAUX] on the amount you invest. Any other cost is listed in the small-print block at the bottom of this page.
Can I change or stop my plan?
Yes. You can change the amount, pause or sell at any time in the app. There is no fixed period unless you pick one yourself.
