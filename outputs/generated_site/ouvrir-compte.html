Frequently asked questions

How long does opening an account take?
About [DUREE] online if you have your ID and your phone with you. If we need more from you, the screen tells you before you finish.
Can I open an account without going to a branch?
Yes. The whole application runs on your phone or your computer. If you would rather speak to someone, you can book an appointment.
What do I need to have at hand?
Your Belgian eID or your residence permit, your national register number and a mobile number. Nothing to print, nothing to post.
I already have an account elsewhere. Can I still open one here?
Yes. You do not have to close your old account first, and you can move your income and bills over when you are ready.
How is my account kept safe?
You confirm logins and payments with itsme, your eID or the app. You set your own transfer limits and change them whenever you want.
