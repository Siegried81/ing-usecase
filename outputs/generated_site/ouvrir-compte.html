Frequently asked questions

What do I need to open an account?
Your identity card or passport, your national register number, your address in Belgium and a phone or tablet with a camera. The whole application runs online.
How long does the application take?
About [DUREE] if your documents are ready. You can stop halfway and continue later on the same device.
Is the account free for students?
It is free while you are enrolled in a recognised course and can show your enrolment certificate. Send it once a year. Once you stop studying, the standard monthly fee of [MONTANT] applies.
Can I open an ING account if I already bank somewhere else?
Yes. You can run an ING account next to your current one. When you are ready, you move your salary and your direct debits across.
What if something goes wrong during the application?
Call us. Someone walks you through the step you are on and stays with you until it is done.
