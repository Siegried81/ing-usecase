Frequently asked questions

Can I open an account without going to a branch?
Yes. You open your account online from your own phone or computer, and you confirm your identity with your eID or your passport. If you prefer to talk it through first, you can book an appointment at a branch and we will go through the steps with you.
What do I need before I start?
Your eID card and PIN, or your passport. Your phone, to confirm your identity. Your address and your email address. Nothing else is needed.
How long does the application take?
About [DUREE] minutes. You can stop halfway and continue later without starting again. Your account number appears on screen when you sign, and your card arrives within [DUREE].
What does the account cost?
[MONTANT] per month, covering your account, your card and your online banking. The full conditions are shown next to the button that starts your application, before you commit to anything.
I stopped in the middle of the application. Do I have to begin again?
No. Your application is saved. Sign in again with your eID or your passport and continue from the step where you stopped.
