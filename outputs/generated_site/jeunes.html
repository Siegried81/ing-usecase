A first account in your child's name

The Youth account is a current account for a child or a student. You open it as a parent or guardian, and the account stays linked to you.

Your child gets a debit card and access to the app. You keep oversight of what comes in and what goes out.

  • A Belgian account number in your child's name
  • A debit card for everyday payments
  • Cash withdrawals in Belgium and abroad
  • Opened and supervised by you, the parent

How you open it, step by step

You open the account, not your child. You sign, and your child co-signs the parts that concern them.

Have your own identity card and your child's details with you. You can start the application online and an adviser confirms the details with you, or arrange everything at a branch.

  • Your identity card
  • Your child's details: name, date of birth, address
  • A short conversation with an adviser if you want one
  • The account number follows within a few working days

You stay in control while your child learns

Your child uses the card and the app, and you keep the final say. You set the limits, you see the movements, and you can step in when something goes wrong.

As your child gets older, you hand over more. The account grows with them.

  • You set the limits on payments and cash withdrawals

Frequently asked questions

Who can open a Youth account?
A parent or legal guardian opens the account for a child. You sign as the adult, and the account is in your child's name.
What do I need to have at hand?
Your own identity card and your child's details. If you start online, an adviser confirms the rest with you.
Can I limit what my child spends?
Yes. You set the limits for card payments and cash withdrawals, and you can change them later. You can also block the card if it is lost.
What happens when my child grows up?
The account can move fully into your child's name when they are ready to manage it themselves. Ask your adviser when you open the account.
