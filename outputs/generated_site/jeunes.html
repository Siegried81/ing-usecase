Who it is for, and what it costs

A youth account is for a child or a student. You open it as a parent or guardian, and you stay the adult contact on the account until your child is ready to take it over.

Your child gets a debit card in their own name. They pay in shops, take out cash, and follow every payment in the ING app.

The conditions, in plain words

You remain the adult contact. That means you can follow the account, set the limit and close it if your situation changes.

Your child takes over more of the account as they get older, with your agreement. When they stop studying, we move them to an adult account so they do not start again from zero.

The age limits, the fee and the full conditions are set out in the documents you receive before you open the account.

  • You stay the adult contact at the start
  • Your child can take over the running of the account later
  • Moving to an adult account is a single step we handle with you

Frequently asked questions

Who can open a youth account?
A parent or guardian opens it for a child or a student. You need your own ID and your child's national register number. The age limits are set out in the terms you receive before you open the account.
What does it cost?
The fee is [MONTANT] a month. You see that figure before you confirm anything, and it is the same whether your child uses the account every day or once a month.
Can I follow what my child spends?
Yes. You can see every payment in the app, set a weekly limit, and freeze the card if it goes missing. You stay the adult contact on the account.
Does my child get a card in their own name?
Yes. The card carries your child's name, and they can pay and take out cash with it. You keep the view of the account and the controls.
What happens when my child stops studying?
We help them move to an adult account. You stay involved in that step, and the change is explained to you both before it happens.
