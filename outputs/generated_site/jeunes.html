Frequently asked questions

Who can open a Youth account?
A parent or legal guardian opens it for a child or a student. Your child does not have to be there when you do it.
How long do the youth conditions apply?
The youth conditions apply for [DUREE]. Before that period ends, we explain the options so you and your child can choose what comes next.
What do we need to open it?
You need your own identity document and your child's identity details. If anything else is needed in your situation, we say so during the application.
Can my child pay online and follow the balance?
The account comes with a debit card and access to the ING app, so your child can pay in shops and online and see the balance. You keep oversight as the parent.
