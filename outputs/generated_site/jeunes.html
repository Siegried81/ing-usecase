You open it. Your child uses it.

An account for your child starts with you. You apply as the parent or legal guardian, in your own name, and your child's name goes on the account.

Your child then uses the card and the app in their daily life: paying at school, topping up a phone, saving for something. You set the limits and you can change them whenever you want.

You never hand over control by accident. Everything that changes on the account, you confirm first.

The account grows with your child

A young child needs something different from a teenager. Access grows step by step. First a card, later the app, later their own login.

You decide when each step happens. We tell you what changes and what it means, in plain words, before it happens.

Interest on the account is [TAUX]. The conditions that apply to that rate are shown next to it, in the same place, so nothing is hidden further down.

  • A card first, app access later.
  • You switch each step on when your child is ready.
  • No change happens without your confirmation.

You keep the overview

You see the account in your own ING app, next to your other accounts. Payments, balances, limits: one screen, no separate login.

You can pause card payments, lower a limit or top up the account in a few taps. Useful when a card goes missing, or when a child needs a reminder about a budget.

If your child turns 18, we tell you in time what changes and what you need to do, so the move to their own name is planned and not a surprise.

  • Every payment visible in your app.

  • Freeze the card or turn payments off in one tap.

Frequently asked questions

Who opens the account?
You do. A parent or legal guardian applies for the account. Your child's name goes on it, and your child uses the card and the app with the access you allow.
What does it cost?
The account costs [MONTANT] per month. You see the price and the conditions in plain language before you confirm anything, and you can stop at any point in the application.
How much can my child spend?
You set the limits yourself, up to [MONTANT]. You can lower or raise them whenever you want, and card payments can be turned off completely.
Can I see what happens on the account?
Yes. The account appears in your own ING app together with your other accounts. You see every payment and every top-up, without asking your child for anything.
What happens when my child turns 18?
You get a message [DUREE] in advance explaining what changes. Together you decide when the account moves into your child's own name, and we guide you through the steps.
