Frequently asked questions

Can my child open the account alone?
No. A parent or guardian opens it and stays the account holder. Your child gets the card and the app, and can see the balance and the payments on the account.
What does the youth account cost?
There is no monthly account fee for the first [DUREE], on the condition that the account stays in your name for that period. After that the monthly account fee is [MONTANT]. The card, the app and the payments are included.
Can I change or stop the account later?
Yes. You can lower a limit, pause the card or close the account at any time in the app. Nothing happens without your confirmation.
What happens when my child is ready to manage the account alone?
You tell us, and we walk you through moving the account into your child's name. Until then, nothing changes.
