Frequently asked questions

Who opens the youth account?
A parent or legal guardian opens it for a child or student. The account is in the young person's name, and you keep access to the overview.
How long does the sign-up take?
Usually a few minutes. You do it on your phone, confirm your identity in the app and add your child's details. You can pause and continue later.
Can I follow what my child spends?
Yes. You see the transactions on the account in your app, and you can set or change the spending limits. Your child sees their own balance separately.
Is there a card with the account?
Yes, a debit card in the young person's name. Any fees linked to the card are listed in the price list.
What happens when the young person turns 18?
The account can move to a standard account. We tell you in advance and explain the steps, so nothing changes without you knowing.
