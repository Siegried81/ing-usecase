Switching: open first, close later

You do not close your old account on day one. Open your ING account, move your salary and your direct debits across, then close the old one when nothing is left running on it. That order keeps you out of trouble.

If you are unsure which direct debits will follow you, ask us and we will walk you through the steps.

  • No branch visit at any point.
  • Keep your old account open while you move.
  • Ask us for help whenever you get stuck.

Frequently asked questions

Which pack should I choose?
Start with how you use your account. ING Go covers everyday banking. If you want more included, move up to ING More, ING Extra or ING Max. You can change pack later without opening a new account.
How long does opening an account take?
You apply in the ING app and confirm your identity with itsme or a video call. Most people finish the application in one sitting.
Can I open an account as a student?
Yes. ING Go is made for first accounts. You need proof of identity and an address in Belgium, and you apply from your phone.
What does the account cost?
The monthly pack fee starts at [MONTANT] and is shown next to each pack. Your ING debit card is included in every pack.
What happens to my old account when I switch?
You keep it open until your salary and your direct debits have moved. Close it once nothing is left running on it, and nothing gets paid twice.
