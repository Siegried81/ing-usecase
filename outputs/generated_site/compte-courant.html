Your card and your app, from day one

Your debit card works in Belgium and abroad, and you can block and unblock it yourself in the app at any moment.

In the app you see each payment as it happens, you can set a limit on your card and you can pay with your phone. These are the things the app does today, and you can test them the week your account opens.

  • Block your card in the app, and unblock it the same way.
  • See what you spent this week, sorted by shop or by category.
  • Set a limit on card payments and change it whenever you want.
  • Pay with your phone without taking your card out.

Frequently asked questions

What does a current account cost?

The switch usually takes [DUREE]. Your old account stays open until everything has moved, so your salary and your direct debits keep working.
