What your mortgage costs, in plain numbers

Three numbers decide what you pay: the rate, the monthly amount and the term. We put them together, each with what it means for your budget.

A longer term brings the monthly amount down and the total cost up. Your adviser shows you both sides before you sign anything.

  • Rate [TAUX] – the interest you pay each year on the amount you still owe.
  • Monthly amount [MONTANT] – what leaves your account every month, capital and interest together.
  • Term [DUREE] – how long you keep paying.
  • Total cost – the sum of all your payments over the term, stated in your personal offer.

Fixed, variable or a mix of both

With a fixed rate you pay the same amount every month for the whole term [DUREE]. Your budget is set from day one.

With a variable rate the amount can move with the market. It usually starts lower. [TAUX]

With a mixed loan you start fixed and switch to variable later. Your adviser shows you what the switch means for your monthly amount, before you choose.

  • Fixed – one amount, no movement.
  • Variable – a lower start, and movement later.
  • Mixed – fixed first, then variable.

Frequently asked questions

What is the TAEG?
The TAEG is the annual percentage rate of charge. It brings the interest rate, the file fee and the linked insurance into one figure, so you can compare two offers on the same basis. Your personal TAEG is [TAUX] and appears in your written offer.
How much can I borrow?
That depends on your income, your existing loans, your own contribution and the term you choose. A simulation gives you a realistic range before you commit to anything: [MONTANT].
What do I bring to the first appointment?
Your ID, proof of income for the last few months, an overview of your current loans and savings, and the details of the property if you already have them.
Can I repay early?
Yes. The rules and any fee are in your offer before you sign: [MONTANT]. Ask your adviser to calculate the year you have in mind.
How long does it take?
From a first appointment to a written offer usually takes [DUREE]. We tell you where your file stands at every step.
