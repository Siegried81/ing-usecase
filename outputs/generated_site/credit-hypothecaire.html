Your rate, your term, your monthly payment

A mortgage is built from three choices: how much you borrow, how long you take to repay, and the rate that applies. Together they decide one figure, your monthly payment.

Take a fixed rate and your payment stays the same from the first month to the last. Take a variable rate and your payment follows the market. We explain both in plain numbers at the appointment.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a fixed and a variable rate?
With a fixed rate, your monthly payment stays the same for the whole term. With a variable rate, your payment moves with the market. Your offer shows which one you chose and what it means for your payment.
How much can I borrow?
It depends on your income, your existing commitments and the property you buy. At the appointment we go through your figures and give you a clear answer in writing.
How long does my offer stay valid?
Your offer states its validity period in writing: [DUREE]. The rate in the offer applies for that period. If you need more time, we make a new offer with the rate that applies then.
What do I bring to the appointment?
Your identity card, your last three payslips, your most recent bank statements and the details of the property you want to buy. If you are buying with someone else, you both come.
Does my monthly payment include insurance?
The annual percentage rate of charge includes the insurance linked to the loan when you take it with us. Your offer states exactly which elements your payment includes.
