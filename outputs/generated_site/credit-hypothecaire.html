Fixed or variable: you choose what suits your plans

With a fixed rate, your monthly payment stays the same for the whole term. You know today what you pay in five, ten or twenty years.

With a variable rate, your payment follows the market. It can go down, and it can go up.

Not sure which one fits? Your adviser runs both through your budget with you and tells you what each one means for your month.

Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow?
It depends on your income, your existing loans, the price of the property and the term you choose. You can see an indication before the appointment, and you get your own figure when we look at your file together.
What is the APR?
The APR is the annual percentage rate of charge. It is the single figure that combines the interest with the costs of your loan. Your offer shows it as [TAUX] for your file, next to the total amount you repay.
What do I bring to the first appointment?
Your ID, your last three payslips, an overview of your savings and any loans you already have, and the listing or the details of the property you have in mind. If you buy with someone else, bring their documents too.
How long does it take from an accepted offer to the signing?
Count on [DUREE] before the deed at the notary. That period covers the legal checks and the preparation of your file. Your adviser gives you the dates as they are confirmed.
