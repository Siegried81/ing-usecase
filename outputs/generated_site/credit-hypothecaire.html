Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow?
It depends on your income, your savings and the property. At your appointment we go through the numbers and you get one clear answer: how much, over [DUREE], at [TAUX]. Asking costs nothing.
What is the TAEG?
The TAEG is the annual percentage rate of charge. It brings the interest and the costs you have to take with the mortgage into one percentage, so you can compare two offers on the same basis. You find it on your personalised offer.
What do I bring to the appointment?
Your identity card, your recent payslips, or your company accounts if you are self-employed, and any details of the property you have in mind. If something is missing, we tell you what to send afterwards.
Can I repay early or pay a little extra each month?
Ask your adviser about the conditions for early repayment. They are set out in your offer before you sign, so you know where you stand in advance.
How long does it take?
Timing depends on your file and on the property. Your adviser gives you the dates in writing, from the first appointment to the signature at the notary.
