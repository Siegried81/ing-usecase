From the first conversation to the keys

We start with your budget. You leave that conversation knowing how much you can borrow and what it costs you.

Then we look at the property. An appraiser visits, we confirm the figures with you, and your credit agreement is drawn up.

After that, the notary takes care of the transfer and the registration of the mortgage. You sign, and the home is yours.

One adviser follows your file from beginning to end. The same person, the same number, from your first question to the last signature.

Frequently asked questions

What is the annual percentage rate of charge?
It is the total cost of your mortgage expressed as a yearly percentage. It covers the interest and the costs of the loan. For your file it is [TAUX], and you find it in writing in your offer.
How much can I borrow?
That depends on your income, your savings and the period you repay over: [DUREE]. We calculate it with you, and you leave the appointment with a written estimate.
Can I repay my mortgage early?
Yes. An early repayment charge may apply, and the amount is [MONTANT]. The conditions sit in your credit agreement, and your adviser shows you the figure before you decide.
What do I bring to the appointment?
Your ID, proof of income, an overview of your savings and any other loans, and the details of the property if you have found one. If you are buying together, you each bring your own documents.
