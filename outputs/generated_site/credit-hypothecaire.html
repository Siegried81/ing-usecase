From first conversation to your signature

What shapes your rate

Your rate depends on the term you choose, the size of your own contribution and whether you take a fixed or a variable rate. We show you the effect of each choice rather than one figure with no context.

If your situation changes before you sign, tell us. The offer can be looked at again.

  • Fixed rate: the same monthly payment for the whole term
  • Variable rate: follows the market, so your payment can move
  • A longer term lowers the monthly payment and raises the total cost

Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow?
It depends on your income, your existing credit and your own contribution. An adviser goes through your situation with you during the appointment and gives you a figure you can plan with.
What is the APR, and why is it higher than my rate?
The APR brings the costs of the credit together with the rate into one percentage, so you can compare two offers on total cost rather than on the headline rate alone. We always show it beside the rate: [TAUX].
What does the first appointment cost?
Nothing. The first conversation is free and commits you to nothing. You leave with a clear view of what you can borrow and what it would cost each month.
How long does the whole thing take?
From the first appointment to the signature, most files take around [DUREE]. The exact timing depends on the property and on how quickly the documents come together. Your adviser keeps you informed at each stage.
Can I pay off early?
Yes. You can make extra payments or pay off the credit early. Ask your adviser which conditions and costs apply to your mortgage.
