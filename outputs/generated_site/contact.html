Frequently asked questions

How quickly will I get an answer?
In the app, a chat is usually picked up within [DUREE]. By phone, you speak to an adviser during opening hours. A written question gets an answer within [DUREE]. These times apply on working days and count from the moment your message reaches us.
Can I arrange everything by phone, or do I need the app?
Day-to-day actions run in the app: blocking a card, disputing a payment, transferring money, downloading your statements. A few changes, such as closing an account, need written confirmation. An adviser tells you what applies to your case.
I have lost my card. What do I do first?
Block it in the app. That works at any hour and takes a moment. Then call us, so we can order a replacement and go through the recent payments with you. A replacement card costs [MONTANT].
Can I visit a branch without an appointment?
Yes. For advice on a mortgage, an investment or opening an account, an appointment saves you waiting. Book it in the app or by phone, and bring your identity card.
Do I pay to call ING?
Call charges depend on your operator. If you would rather not call, send your question in the app and we answer in writing.
