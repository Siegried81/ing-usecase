What to have ready before you contact us

A few details up front turn three contacts into one. Keep your customer number, your card and your ID within reach, and tell us which payment or account the question is about.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest way to reach ING?
The app chat. You reach a colleague during opening hours and keep the conversation in one place. For an urgent card matter, call the helpdesk at any hour. For advice, book a branch appointment.
My card is lost or stolen. What do I do first?
Block the card in the app or by phone. Blocking is free of charge. After that, order a replacement in the app and check your recent payments for anything you do not recognise.
Do I pay for calling or chatting with ING?
No. Calls to the helpdesk and chats in the app are free from a Belgian landline or mobile. If you call from outside Belgium, your own operator's rates apply.
Do I need an appointment to visit a branch?
Book one for advice on an account, a savings plan, a mortgage or an investment. For a simple card or access matter, you can walk in during opening hours. Bring your ID.
How long before I get an answer to a message?
A chat is answered while you wait during opening hours. A message sent through the app or the online form gets a reply within [DUREE].
