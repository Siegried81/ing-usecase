Your daily transfer limit, explained

Every account comes with a daily limit on outgoing transfers. Your limit is [MONTANT] until you change it, and you can see the figure that applies to you on the account in the app.

You can change it yourself. Open the account, choose the new limit and confirm with your usual sign-in. For a higher limit we ask you to confirm your identity first, so that nobody else can raise it for you.

  • Lower your limit at any time, it takes effect straight away
  • Raise it and confirm with your usual sign-in
  • If a limit is blocking a payment you need to make, message us before the payment is due

Security and access, in plain words

We write about security and access without hurry. No countdown stands between you and your money, and no message asks you to act within minutes. If something needs your attention, you will find it in the app when you sign in.

We never ask for your PIN or your sign-in codes, by phone, by e-mail or in a message. If someone does, end the contact and report it to us from the app.

Lost your card or locked out of your account? Report it in the app or call us. We can block your card and your access and order a new card, without you coming to a branch.

  • Check messages about your account in the app, not through a link
  • Never share your PIN or your sign-in codes
  • Report a lost card or a suspicious message from the app
  • Trouble signing in? Reset your access in the app or call us

Frequently asked questions

What is my daily transfer limit, and how do I change it?
Every account has a daily limit on outgoing transfers. Yours is [MONTANT] until you change it, and you can see it on the account in the app. To lower it, change it in the app and it applies straight away. To raise it, confirm with your usual sign-in; for a higher limit we first ask you to confirm your identity.
How do I reach a person?
Send a message in the ING Banking app and it goes to the team that handles your question. You can also call us during opening hours or book an appointment at a branch. The current phone number and opening hours are listed in the app.
How do I check that a message really comes from ING?
Sign in to the app and look for the message there. We contact you through the app or through the details you have registered with us. We never ask for your PIN or your sign-in codes, and we never ask you to move money to a safe account.
My card is lost, or I cannot get into my account. What do I do?
Report it in the app or call us. We can block your card and your access and order a new card. You do not have to come to a branch for this.
Can I get help without coming to a branch?
Yes. Most questions are handled in the app, in writing, so you keep a record of the answer. If you prefer to talk in person, book an appointment at a branch and we will have your details ready.
