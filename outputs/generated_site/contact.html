What to have ready before you call

What happens after you contact us

A chat connects you to an advisor within [DUREE]. A message you send outside opening hours gets an answer on the next working day.

That means you can ask your question in one short message and get on with your day. If we cannot answer straight away, we tell you who will follow up and when.

Frequently asked questions

How do I speak to a real person?
Start a chat or call us on [MONTANT]. Both routes put you with an advisor. You do not have to pass a menu or repeat your question twice.
I lost my card. What do I do first?
Block it in the app or call us on [MONTANT]. The block takes effect immediately. You can order a new card at the same time, and it arrives by post within [DUREE].
Can I ask about savings, a term account or a mortgage here?
Yes. The advisor who picks up your chat or call can talk you through savings and current accounts. For a mortgage, we book you a call with a specialist who can look at your situation in detail.
I am not a customer yet. Can I still contact you?
Yes. Ask your question first and decide afterwards. If you already know what you want, you can open an account online and contact us if anything is unclear.
In which languages can I contact you?
You can write or speak to us in Dutch, French or English. Say which language you prefer at the start of the chat or call.
