Three ways to reach a person

Chat is the quickest route. You type your question, a colleague answers, and the conversation stays in your app so you can pick it up again later.

Call us when the question needs a voice, or when something has to be sorted out straight away. If you would rather sit down with someone, book a slot in a branch and you are seen at the time you chose.

  • Chat in the app: account questions, cards and payments
  • By phone: anything urgent, including a card you have lost
  • In a branch: paperwork, signatures and bigger decisions

If something is wrong, act first

A lost card, a payment you do not recognise, a message asking for your codes: act first, then talk to us. You can freeze your card yourself in the app, at any hour, and unfreeze it just as easily once you have found it.

We never ask for your PIN, your codes or your card number by email, text or phone. If someone does, it is not us.

  • Lost or stolen card: freeze it in the app
  • Suspicious message: do not tap the link, report it to us
  • A payment you do not recognise: check the details, then contact us

Frequently asked questions

How quickly will I get an answer?
In chat, within [DUREE]. Messages that arrive outside our opening hours are answered on the next working day. Urgent card matters are handled first, whatever route you use.
Can I talk to someone in my own language?
Yes. We help you in Dutch, French or English. Say which language you prefer at the start of the chat or the call, and we continue in that language.
What should I have ready before I call?
Your customer number, the card or account your question is about, and the reference of any earlier message. That saves a round of questions before we can help.
I think a message is a scam. What do I do?
Do not open the link and do not share any code. Send the message to us through the app or call us, and we look at it with you. We never ask for your PIN or your codes.
Can I come to a branch?
Yes. Book a slot first so you are seen at the time you chose, and bring your ID and any document the appointment is about. For quick questions, chat is usually faster.
