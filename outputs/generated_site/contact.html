Frequently asked questions

How do I block a lost or stolen card?
Open the ING app, go to Card settings and block the card. The block takes effect straight away. You can request a replacement in the same place, and we show you the cost before you confirm.
How quickly will I get an answer?
Messages in the app are answered in the order they arrive. If your card is blocked, a payment is wrong or you think someone has your codes, use the urgent route in the app or call us instead of waiting for a written reply.
Can I open an account or apply for a mortgage without coming to a branch?
Yes. Most applications start online or in the app. Have your ID card ready, and for a mortgage your income details. If you would rather talk it through, book an appointment and we confirm a time with you.
Someone asked for my PIN or my codes. What should I do?
Do not share them. ING never asks for your PIN or access codes by phone, email or text. End the conversation, then contact us so we can check your account and secure it.
What happens to the data I give you when I contact you?
We use it to handle your question, to run your account and to meet our legal duties. How we handle personal data is set out in our privacy notice, which you receive when you take out a product.
