Frequently asked questions

What does a free account actually cost me?
Nothing to open, and nothing monthly as long as you keep to the conditions shown next to each free item. Where a condition applies, such as keeping the account open for [DUREE], you see it on the same line as the word free.
Can I open an account from my phone?
Yes. Fill in your details, confirm your identity in the app, and the account is open. Your card arrives by post and the app works the same day.
Which pack should I choose?
Start with the entry pack if you want one card and the app. Move up if you want a second card or the extra services. You can change pack later.
