Frequently asked questions

What rate will I get on my savings?
Your savings account pays [TAUX]. You see the rate before you open, with the date it applies from. Rates can change over time, and the rate in force for your account is always the one shown in your app and on ing.be.
Can I take my money out when I want?
Yes. A savings account has no fixed term. You can withdraw when you need to, and add money again later. Your account terms explain exactly how a withdrawal affects the interest for that period.
What do I need to open an account?
Your ID and your phone. You apply online and sign digitally. If you are new to ING, we check your details as part of the application.
Is my money safe with ING?
Your savings are held in Belgium and covered by the Belgian deposit protection scheme. The amount covered, [MONTANT] per person, and the conditions are set out in the legal information that comes with your account.
