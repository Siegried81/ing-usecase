Put your savings to work with a term account

A fixed rate for a fixed term, so you know exactly where you stand.

Open a term account

You have worked for your savings. So it makes sense that you want them to work for you too. A term account does exactly that: you set your money aside for a period you choose, and in return you get a rate that is agreed upfront.

That means no surprises. You know the rate from day one, and you know it will not move until your term ends. Whether you are saving for a home, a sabbatical or simply a little more peace of mind, you can plan around a figure that stays put.

Opening one is straightforward. You decide how much you want to put in and for how long, and we take care of the rest. There is no need to keep an eye on it, and no need to move money around to chase a better deal.

You can open a term account online in a few minutes. If you would rather talk it through first, you can reach us by phone or drop into a branch. Either way, you will get a clear answer, not a sales pitch.

Your money stays yours. You can see your term account in the same app as the rest of your banking, so you always have the full picture. And when your term comes to an end, we will let you know in good time so you can decide what happens next.

Rates depend on the amount you deposit and the term you choose. You will see the rate that applies to you before you confirm anything, so you can compare and decide at your own pace.
